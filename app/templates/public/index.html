A word from our pastor

We are more than a church. We are a family rooted in the Word of God, dedicated to serving Accra and the nations.

Welcome to Triumphant Assemblies of God! We are delighted to have you join our church family. Our mission is to lead individuals into a life-changing relationship with Jesus Christ, nurture them in faith and community, and equip them to serve with purpose.

Whether you are seeking spiritual growth, community fellowship, or simply a place to worship, we invite you to experience the warmth, love, and spiritual power that defines our church. Together, we are building a thriving, Spirit-empowered community that mirrors the first church.
