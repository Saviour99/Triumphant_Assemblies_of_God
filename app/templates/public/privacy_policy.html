1. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you interact with the Site:

Member Account

When you register as a member, we collect your full name, email address, phone number, and a password (stored as a secure one-way hash — we never store your password in plain text). You may optionally upload a profile avatar.

Prayer Requests & Contact Messages

When you submit a prayer request or use our contact form, we collect your name, email address, and the message content (including the topic or subject you provide).

Newsletter Subscription

When you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address.

Giving / Donations

When you give online, we collect your name, email address, donation amount, and the fund you're giving toward (e.g. tithe, offering, missions, building fund). See Giving & Payments below for how card details are handled.

Automatically Collected Information

Like most websites, our server may log standard technical data such as IP address and browser type for security and troubleshooting purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your member account and profile
  • Respond to prayer requests and contact/general inquiries
  • Send the newsletter to subscribers who opt in
  • Process and record donations, and maintain your giving history if you're a logged-in member
  • Maintain the security of the Site, including detecting and preventing abuse
  • Communicate important updates about church events, services, or your account

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your data for third-party advertising.

3. Giving & Payments

Online giving is processed through Paystack, a third-party payment processor. When you make a donation, your card details are entered directly into Paystack's secure payment popup and are never transmitted to or stored on our servers. We only receive and store the donation amount, fund type, donor name/email, and a payment reference/status (pending, completed, or failed) used to confirm your transaction. Paystack's own privacy policy governs how they handle your payment card data.

4. Cookies & Session Data

We use a session cookie to keep you signed in after you log in as a member or admin, and a CSRF token to protect forms on the Site from cross-site request forgery. These cookies are essential to the Site's functioning and are not used for advertising or cross-site tracking. We do not currently use third-party analytics or advertising cookies.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share information only in these limited circumstances:

  • With Paystack, solely to process a donation you initiate
  • With church staff/administrators who need it to respond to your request, manage your membership, or record your giving
  • Where required to comply with a legal obligation, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of TAG, our members, or the public

6. Data Retention

We retain member account data for as long as your account remains active. Prayer requests, contact messages, and donation records are retained as part of the church's ongoing pastoral and financial records. You may request deletion of your member account at any time as described in Your Rights & Choices below.

7. Your Rights & Choices

  • Access & correction — you can review and update your details from your member profile at any time.
  • Newsletter opt-out — every newsletter email includes an unsubscribe option, or you may email us directly.
  • Account deletion — contact us using the details below to request deletion of your member account and associated personal data, subject to any records we're required to keep for financial or legal reasons.

8. Children's Privacy

The Site is intended for general audiences and member registration is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through the member registration system. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

9. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including password hashing, CSRF protection on all forms, and signature-verified payment webhooks. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Site after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
