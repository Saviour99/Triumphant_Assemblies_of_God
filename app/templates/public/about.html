Our History

The Founding of Triumphant Assemblies of God

The origins of Triumphant Assemblies of God are deeply rooted in faithful service, spiritual vision, and divine direction through the ministry of Rev. Samuel Baah Yeboah. While serving as a lay worker at Exhibition Assemblies of God, he began a prayer fellowship at Kofi ne Ama in Dansoman. What started as a small gathering soon attracted other lay workers and members from his home church who shared a passion for prayer and fellowship. Under the mentorship and spiritual guidance of the late Rev. George Annan, who provided valuable direction on organizing and nurturing the fellowship, the group continued to grow steadily. In 1994, after discerning God’s hand upon the work, Rev. Annan commissioned Rev. Baah Yeboah to establish a new church.
In obedience to this divine assignment, Rev. Baah Yeboah secured a suitable meeting place at the residence of Mr. Stephen Klutse on Gbegbu Road. The early church services were held in a temporary structure on the property. That same year, Rev. Baah Yeboah’s ministerial preparation was further strengthened when he enrolled at the Assemblies of God Theological Seminary, Saltpond.


Despite the demands of theological training, he remained steadfast in leading the fellowship at Mr. Klutse’s residence. The meeting place soon became a vibrant center of worship and spiritual fellowship for believers from the surrounding communities of Mpoase and Glefe. To further support the growing congregation, the late Rev. Annan encouraged members of Exhibition Assemblies of God residing in the area to join and strengthen the fellowship. Following his graduation from the seminary in 1997, Rev. Samuel Baah Yeboah was officially installed as the Pastor of the newly constituted Triumphant Assemblies of God. Under his leadership and through the dedication of the congregation, the church experienced remarkable growth.
A major milestone was achieved just four years later with the construction of the church’s current auditorium. This significant accomplishment provided a permanent place of worship and created a strong foundation for continued fellowship, ministry, and spiritual development. The founding story of Triumphant Assemblies of God stands as a testimony to faith, obedience, sacrifice, and God’s sustaining grace.

Our Vision, Mission & Purpose

What drives us forward

Our Vision

To be a thriving, Spirit-empowered community that mirrors the first church: deeply rooted in God's presence, committed to genuine fellowship, and actively expanding God's Kingdom in our community and beyond.

Our Mission

To lead individuals into a life-changing relationship with Jesus Christ, nurture them in faith and community, and equip them to serve with purpose.

Our Purpose

To glorify God by becoming a beacon of hope, a center for spiritual growth, and a catalyst for transformative love in our families, community, and nation.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide our church

Christ Above All:

Making Jesus the undisputed center of our lives and decisions.

Rooted in Truth

Living with integrity, guided by the authority of Scripture.

Building to Last

Stewarding resources for generational impact and a lasting legacy.

Generous in Service

Driven by Excellence

Empowering Every Leader

Pursuing unity and celebrating diversity as one family in Christ.
