Our History, Vision, Mission & Values

Our History

A Journey of Faith Since 1994

Triumphant Assemblies of God was founded in 1994 with a vision to establish a church that would be a beacon of hope, love, and spiritual transformation in our community. What started as a small gathering of believers has grown into a thriving congregation of over 2,500 members.

Under the visionary leadership of Rev. Rexford Peprah, TAG has become known for its powerful preaching, spirit-filled worship, and compassionate community outreach. Our church has been instrumental in transforming lives, strengthening families, and impacting our society for Christ.

Over the past three decades, we have established multiple ministries, touched thousands of lives, and expanded our facilities to accommodate our growing congregation. We remain committed to our founding principles of faith, love, and purpose.

Our Vision, Mission & Purpose

What drives us forward

Our Vision

To be a Spirit-empowered church that transforms lives, strengthens families, and impacts society with the love and power of Jesus Christ.

Our Mission

To lead individuals into a life-changing relationship with Jesus Christ, nurture them in faith and community, and equip them to serve with purpose and passion.

Our Purpose

To glorify God, grow God's kingdom, and serve humanity with compassion, integrity, and excellence in all we do.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide our church

Faith in Jesus Christ

We believe in the centrality of Jesus Christ and His redemptive work for all humanity.

Love & Compassion

We demonstrate God's love through compassionate service to our community and the world.

Biblical Truth

We are committed to the authority and reliability of God's Word as our foundation for faith and practice.

Community & Fellowship

We value authentic relationships and believe in the power of Christian community and mutual support.

Spirit-Empowered Ministry

We depend on the Holy Spirit's power and guidance for all our ministry and outreach efforts.
