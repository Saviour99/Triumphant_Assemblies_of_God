Jesus is in the Boat

Rev. Rexford Peprah Mark 4:39 April 28, 2024 Devotional

"He got up, rebuked the wind and said to the waves, ‘Quiet! Be still!’ Then the wind died down and it was completely calm."

REFLECTION

The storm was raging, but Jesus was sleeping in the boat. His presence changed everything. When you face overwhelming circumstances, remember that Jesus is with you. He may seem silent, but He is never absent. One word from Him can calm any storm. Do not look at the waves look at the One who is in the boat with you.

REFLECTION

  • Lord, help me to remember that You are with me in every storm.
  • Speak peace over the chaos in my life right now.
  • Increase my faith to trust You even when the waves are high.

DECLARATION

Jesus is in my boat. I will not fear, for He speaks peace over every storm.

JESUS IS LORD
