Welcome Message

Welcome to Our Family in Christ

"We are more than a church — we are a family rooted in the Word of God, dedicated to serving Accra and the nations."

For over 25 years, Triumphant Assemblies of God has been a beacon of hope in Accra, Ghana. Founded on the fire of the Holy Spirit and the uncompromising Word of God, TAG has grown from a small fellowship to a thriving congregation that touches lives across communities.

Whether you are seeking God for the first time, returning to faith, or looking for a church to call home — you are warmly welcomed here. Come as you are. Leave transformed.
